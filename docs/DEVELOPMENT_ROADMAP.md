ECGame – Development Roadmap
Document Version: 1.0.0 Status: Draft Last Updated: August 7, 2026

Purpose
This roadmap aligns the development of ECGame with the Biology 101 curriculum.
Rather than attempting to complete the entire game before instruction begins, ECGame will expand throughout the semester.
New laboratories, mechanics, simulations, and educational activities will be introduced as students reach the corresponding biological concepts.
The laboratory grows with the course.

Development Philosophy
ECGame is designed as an evolving scientific facility.
Students are not expected to master every laboratory during the first week.
Instead, each laboratory opens as new scientific concepts are introduced in class.
This approach offers several advantages:
reduces cognitive overload
reinforces previous concepts
allows incremental software development
enables continuous classroom feedback
permits architectural refinement throughout the semester
The software and curriculum evolve together.

Success Criteria
The roadmap is successful when:
software development follows classroom instruction;
students experience biology as one continuous scientific journey;
each laboratory reinforces previous learning;
new game systems expand existing knowledge rather than replacing it; and
ECGame grows steadily throughout the semester while remaining stable, modular, and enjoyable.
The laboratory is never considered “finished.”
Like science itself, it continually evolves.
