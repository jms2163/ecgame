Stage‑1: Barely Alive Amoeba (ACS‑only metabolism)
This is your “emergency mode.” The cell has:

ACS (ADP‑forming)

CoA pool

ADP pool

Leaky membrane pumps

Minimal cytoskeleton

No glycolysis

No mitochondria

No biosynthesis except the simplest dehydration reactions

Energy trickle:

Acetic acid → acetate → ACS → ATP

This ATP is just enough to:

keep ion pumps barely running

prevent osmotic lysis

allow minimal movement (you can hand‑wave the ATP cost for gameplay)

allow the player to perform very slow dehydration synthesis

allow the cell to repair one or two broken proteins

This is the perfect “survival floor.”

🧭 Movement toward acetate gradients
This is a great mechanic.

Even though real amoebae need ATP for cytoskeleton movement, you can treat movement as “free” for gameplay. Students will intuitively learn:

energy comes from the environment

cells seek out resources

resource gradients matter

This is a simple, powerful teaching tool.

🔧 What players can build with this tiny ATP trickle
You’ve got the right idea: dehydration synthesis is the first “crafting” mechanic.

Players can assemble:

1. Disaccharides
glucose + glucose → maltose

costs ATP

teaches “building molecules requires energy”

2. Fatty acid motifs
acetyl units → short fatty acids

costs ATP

teaches membrane building

3. Amino acid motifs
simple amino acids → dipeptides → tripeptides

costs ATP

teaches protein assembly

These motifs become the “currency” for building enzymes.

🔬 Stage‑2: Rebuilding Glycolysis
This is the first major upgrade.

Players invest motifs to build:

hexokinase

isomerase

aldolase

GAPDH

phosphoglycerate kinase

pyruvate kinase

glucose transporter

Once they assemble these:

Glucose → pyruvate → more ATP
This is the moment students realize:

“Oh… glucose is WAY better than acetate.”

And they feel the difference because ATP production jumps from a trickle to a steady stream.

⚙️ Stage‑3: Organelle Unlocks
This is where your game becomes a puzzle.

Players unlock:

1. Vesicle delivery system
Golgi

ER

membrane trafficking

teaches compartmentalization

2. Peroxisomes
detoxification

fatty acid oxidation

teaches organelle specialization

3. Nucleus repair
transcription

translation

gene regulation

teaches information flow

Each unlock increases ATP demand, forcing players to improve metabolism.

🔥 Stage‑4: Mitochondria (Endgame)
This is the big payoff.

Players assemble:

pyruvate dehydrogenase

TCA cycle enzymes

electron transport chain

ATP synthase

mitochondrial membrane

import/export carriers

Once mitochondria come online:

Respiration → massive ATP
Students feel the evolutionary leap:

“Fermentation was crumbs. Respiration is a feast.”

This is the moment the game teaches the deepest biological truth:
energy availability shapes complexity.

🌍 Stage‑5: Reproduction and Colonization
With abundant ATP, players can:

duplicate DNA

build full membranes

assemble cytoskeleton

divide

colonize new environments

compete for resources

evolve specialization
